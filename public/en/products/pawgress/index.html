What it does

It does four things.

Only the parts you use every day — done properly.

Today's dashboard

See today's tasks and appointments in one list, including what has rolled over, what is urgent and what is waiting on someone else.

WBS and Gantt chart

Break a project into tasks and lay their durations out as bars to see how far along you are. When the dates contradict each other, Pawgress spots it and suggests a fix.

Calendar

Check your schedule by month or by week. Recurring entries, such as a monthly invoice, line themselves up once you have set them (paid edition).

AI chat

Say "meeting next Tuesday" and it is on the calendar. Checking and changing entries works the same way.

Why Pawgress

Where it differs.

Your data stays on your PC. The AI does not just read it, it acts on it

Tasks and schedule are stored on your own PC. The AI chat can create and change them directly, and you decide how far it is allowed to go.

Pawgress decides what you do first today

What is urgent, what you keep putting off, what you can actually move right now. It ranks by those and puts the top five, along with today's schedule, on a single screen.

It finds scheduling contradictions and fixes them in one pass

When parent and child tasks or their order stop adding up, it detects that and shows everything affected. Approve it and the whole set is corrected at once.

What the AI needs

The AI chat connects to an external AI service using an API key you supply yourself. OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and xAI are supported, and any OpenAI-compatible API works, including self-hosted ones (not covered by our support). Your API key is stored on your PC and nowhere else.
