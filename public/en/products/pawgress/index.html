What it does

It does four things.

Just the things you use every day, done well.

Today's dashboard

Today's tasks and events in one list, with what has carried over, what is urgent and what is waiting on others clear at a glance.

WBS and Gantt chart

Break a project into tasks and see their timelines as bars, so you know how far along you are. If dates stop adding up, Pawgress notices and suggests a fix.

Calendar

View your schedule by month or by week. Set up a recurring event, like a monthly invoice, once and it fills itself in from then on (paid edition).

AI chat

Type "meeting next Tuesday" and it's on your calendar. Checking and changing things works the same way.

Why Pawgress

What makes it different.

Your data is stored only on your PC. The AI doesn't just read it — it gets things done

Tasks and events are saved on your own PC and are never sent to us. The AI chat can create and change them directly, and you decide how much it is allowed to do. What you type into the AI chat is sent directly to the AI provider you choose.

Pawgress picks what to do first today

It ranks tasks by what is urgent, what you keep putting off and what you can act on right now, and shows the top five alongside today's schedule on one screen.

It finds scheduling conflicts and fixes them all at once

When parent and child tasks, or tasks that depend on each other, no longer line up, Pawgress flags it and shows everything affected. Approve the fix and it is applied in one go.

What the AI needs

The AI chat connects to an outside AI service using your own API key. The providers and models we have tested are listed below. You can also connect to any OpenAI-compatible API, including one you host yourself, but we have not tested these. Your API key is stored only on your PC.

What the AI costs

You pay the AI provider directly, under your own agreement with them. These fees are not included in the price of Pawgress.

Upgrading from the free edition

No need to uninstall. Install the paid edition over the free one and your existing tasks and events carry over as they are. If you uninstall first, your data is deleted.
