Lucid Brain ("we" or "us") complies with Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information (the "APPI") and related laws, and handles the personal information we deal with in our business as described below.

Article 1. Scope

  1. This policy applies to personal information we handle in our business. Our business includes running our website (the pages under https://lucidbrain.jp/, "this site"), selling and providing our products and services, answering enquiries, and contract development, consulting and other dealings.
  2. How data is handled in our products and services is set out in Article 4, or in the explanation we give for each product or service (such as an appendix to this policy or a notice inside the product) ("product-specific explanations").
  3. Terms used in this policy, such as "personal information", "personal data", "retained personal data", "personally referable information", "pseudonymously processed information" and "anonymously processed information", have the meanings given in the APPI.

Article 7. Sharing with third parties and outsourcing

  1. We do not give personal data to third parties without your prior consent, except where the law requires or the APPI otherwise allows it.
  2. If our business is succeeded to by someone else (including when we incorporate), we may pass personal data to the successor along with the business. Under the APPI, this is not giving data to a third party.
  3. We may outsource all or part of the handling of personal data to other companies to the extent needed for the purposes of use. If we do, we supervise them as needed to keep the data secure.

Article 9. Security measures

To prevent personal data from being leaked, lost or damaged, we take the following measures:

  • Organisational and human measures: only the business owner, and people who develop and run this site and our products under the owner's direction, handle personal data, and we have set rules for how it is handled.
  • Physical measures: we protect the devices used to handle personal data against theft and loss, and protect them with passwords or similar.
  • Technical measures: we use two-factor authentication on our payment, hosting and email accounts and limit who can access them. Our technical records of how this site runs are designed not to include names, email addresses or IP addresses.
  • Understanding the external environment: as described in Article 8, we have looked into the systems of the countries where personal data is handled.

Article 10. Retention and deletion

  1. We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes of use, and delete it when it is no longer needed.
  2. Information the law requires us to keep (such as payment records) is kept for the period the law sets.

Article 11. Your requests

  1. Under the APPI, you can ask us, about the retained personal data we hold about you, to tell you the purposes of use, disclose it, correct, add to or delete it, stop using or erase it, stop giving it to third parties, and disclose our records of giving it to third parties.
  2. Send your request by email to the contact in Article 14. When we receive it, we will explain how to make the request, how we confirm your identity (including when an agent makes the request for you), and any other steps.
  3. We do not charge a fee for these requests.

Article 12. Region and language

  1. We sell our products only to people who live in Japan.
  2. The Japanese version of this policy is the authoritative one. English and other versions are summaries for reference; where they differ, the Japanese version prevails.

Article 13. Changes to this policy

  2. Changes take effect when published on this site. Where the law requires us to notify you or get your consent (such as when changing the purposes of use), we will do so as the law requires.
